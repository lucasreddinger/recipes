Fills a large (8~qt) Instant Pot to about 6~quarts.

\subsection*{Ingredients}

% tight vertical spacing for this block only
{\setlength{\parskip}{0.25\baselineskip}\small

\begin{paracol}{3}
\textbf{Essential} \par
2~lb dry beans (great northern or mix) \par
2--3~large onions, diced \par
3--4~carrots, diced \par
3--4~celery stalks, diced \par
4--8~garlic cloves, minced \par
3--4~medium potatoes, diced \par
3--4~Tbsp veg. Better Than Bouillon \par
2--3~Tbsp olive or avocado oil \par
\switchcolumn
\textbf{Recommended} \par
8--12~oz mushrooms, chopped \par
1--2~Tbsp gochugaru \par
3--4~Tbsp MCT oil \par
2--3~Tbsp liquid aminos \par
Several bay leaves \par
Apple cider vinegar \par
\switchcolumn
\textbf{Nice to have} \par
2--3~Tbsp tomato paste \par
Arugula \par
Thyme, oregano \par
Miso paste \par
Greens (\textit{e.g.}, beet, dandelion) \par
Smoked paprika \par
\end{paracol}
}

\subsection*{Steps}

\paragrcompact{Prep} Rinse beans and remove any junk (\textit{e.g.}, rocks). Rinse and cut other ingredients as desired.

\paragrcompact{Sauté aromatics} Set Instant Pot to sauté. Add oil. Sweat onions, garlic, and half of the carrots and celery for 5--10~minutes (do not brown). \textit{Optional:} Add mushrooms and cook 2--3~minutes more. Stir in tomato paste and toast for 2~minutes.

\paragrcompact{Build base} Add beans, gochugaru, bouillon. \textit{Optional:} Add herbs and smoked paprika for smoky depth (but finish with additional acid). Add enough water to reach the 6~quart mark. Add liquid aminos. Stir to combine. Count and lay bay leaves on top.

\paragrcompact{Pressure cook} Cook on high pressure for 35--40~minutes. Allow natural release at least 20~minutes, then quick release any remaining pressure.

\paragrcompact{Cook remaining veggies} Stir. Add potatoes and remaining carrot and celery. Set to sauté or slow cook for 15--20~minutes, until potatoes are just tender. Season to taste.

\paragrcompact{Finish} Stir in MCT oil. Mash some beans or potatoes against the side if the soup is too brothy. Add salt as needed.

\paragrcompact{Serve} Add black pepper and vinegar. \textit{Optional:} Add arugula.

\subsection*{Notes}

\paragrcompact{Substitutions} Arugula: kale or chard. Vinegar: lemon juice. Liquid aminos: soy sauce. Gochugaru: 2--3~banana peppers, chopped.
