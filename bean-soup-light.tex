\vspace{\baselineskip}

Fills a large (8~qt) Instant Pot to about 6 quarts.

\section{Ingredients}

\begin{paracol}{2}
2~lb dry beans (great northern or 7-bean mix) \par
2--3 large onions, diced \par
3--4 carrots, diced \par
3--4 celery stalks, diced \par
4--8 garlic cloves, minced \par
3--4 medium waxy potatoes, diced \par
2--3 banana peppers, chopped or 1--2~Tbsp gochugaru \par
8--12~oz mushrooms, chopped \par
\switchcolumn
2--3~Tbsp tomato paste \par
3--4~Tbsp vegetable Better Than Bouillon \par
2--3~Tbsp liquid aminos or soy sauce \par
2--3~Tbsp olive or avocado oil \par
Several bay leaves, thyme, oregano \par
3--4~Tbsp MCT oil \par
\end{paracol}

\section{Instructions}

\paragrcompact{Prep} Rinse beans and remove any junk (\textit{e.g.}, rocks). Rinse and cut other ingredients as desired.

\paragrcompact{Sauté aromatics} Set Instant Pot to sauté. Add olive or avocado oil. Sweat onions, garlic, and half of the carrots and celery for 5--10 minutes (do not brown). Add mushrooms and cook 2--3 minutes more. Stir in tomato paste and toast for 2 minutes.

\paragrcompact{Build base} Add beans, banana peppers or gochugaru, herbs (if using), bouillon, and enough water to reach the 6 quart mark. Add liquid aminos or soy sauce. Stir to combine. Count and lay bay leaves on top.

\paragrcompact{Pressure cook} Cook on high pressure for 35--40 minutes. Allow natural release at least 20 minutes, then quick release any remaining pressure.

\paragrcompact{Add potatoes and finish} Stir. Add potatoes and remaining carrot and celery. Set to sauté or slow cook for 15--20 minutes, until potatoes are just tender. Season to taste.

\paragrcompact{Finish and adjust} Stir in MCT oil. Mash some beans or potatoes against the side if the soup is too brothy.  Add salt as needed.

\section{Notes}

\paragrcompact{Serving} Finish with black pepper and vinegar or lemon juice. Consider adding arugula, kale, or chard.

\paragrcompact{Variations} Add smoked paprika for depth, but balance by finishing the soup with additional acid.
