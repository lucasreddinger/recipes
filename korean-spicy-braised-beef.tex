\subsection*{Notes}

\paragrcompact{Beef cut} I like top sirloin, sirloin tip, and similar lean beef because they are relatively low in fat and stay in solid chunks. If you want beef that is more tender, use chuck roast or another fattier braising cut.

\paragrcompact{Heat} Add more Thai chiles for more heat.
