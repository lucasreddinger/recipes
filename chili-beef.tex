Basic chili con carne.

\subsection*{Steps}

\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.15}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X l@{}}
\toprule
Set Instant Pot to Saute on medium. Add oil. While oil heats, chop or dice onions (use food processor if desired). Once oil shimmers, add onions and saute a few minutes.
  &
  \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}R{2em}@{\hspace{0.3em}}l@{\hspace{0.8em}}l@{}}
  2 & ct & med/lg yellow onions \\
  2 & T & neutral oil (e.g., avocado) \\
  \end{tabular}
\\
\midrule
Mince garlic and chilis (food processor recommended). Add to pot and saute a few minutes.
  &
  \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}R{2em}@{\hspace{0.3em}}l@{\hspace{0.8em}}l@{}}
  1 & head & garlic \\
  6 & ct & serrano chili peppers \\
  \end{tabular}
\\
\midrule
Add to pot and saute a few minutes.\\\textit{Can substitute cayenne pepper (maybe 1--2~T?) or gochugaru (maybe 3~T?) in place of serranos.}
  &
  \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}R{2em}@{\hspace{0.3em}}l@{\hspace{0.8em}}l@{}}
  4 & T & chili powder \\
  2 & T & ground cumin \\
  3 & T & mexican oregano \\
  2 & t & sea salt \\
  1 & t & ground black pepper \\
  \end{tabular}
\\
\midrule
Add to pot using hot water to deglaze and clean spoon used for bouillon.
  &
  \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}R{2em}@{\hspace{0.3em}}l@{\hspace{0.8em}}l@{}}
  4 & T & beef Better-Than-Bouillon \\
  10 & c & boiling water \\
  \end{tabular}
\\
\midrule
Pick out crummy beans and rinse well. Add to pot with diced tomato and tomato sauce. Stir. Lay bay leaves on top (count them so you can remove later).
  &
  \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}R{2em}@{\hspace{0.3em}}l@{\hspace{0.8em}}l@{}}
  2 & lbs & beans of choice \\
  14 & fl oz & diced tomato (canned or fresh) \\
  14 & fl oz & tomato sauce (canned) \\
  8 & ct & bay leaves \\
  \end{tabular}
\\
\midrule
Cancel saute. Secure lid. Switch to vent, then to seal. Cook on high pressure for 1~hour. Allow natural release for 30~minutes. Then cancel and remove lid. Pick out bay leaves.
  &
  \mbox{}
\\
\midrule
Saute on high until desired consistency is reached.
  &
  \mbox{}
\\
\midrule
Meanwhile, brown ground beef separately. Drain fat and add to pot.
  &
  \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}R{2em}@{\hspace{0.3em}}l@{\hspace{0.8em}}l@{}}
  2 & lbs & ground beef \\
  \end{tabular}
\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\end{center}
