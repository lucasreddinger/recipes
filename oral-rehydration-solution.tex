Low-osmolarity is strongly recommended for beginners.

\subsection*{Ingredients}

% tight vertical spacing for this block only
{\setlength{\parskip}{0.25\baselineskip}\small

\begin{paracol}{3}
\textbf{Standard formulation (1~L)} \par
3.5~g NaCl (salt) \par
2.9~g Trisodium citrate dihydrate \par
1.5~g KCl (NoSalt) \par
20.0~g Anhydrous glucose \par
27.9~g Total \par
\switchcolumn
\textbf{Low-osmolarity formulation (1~L)} \par
2.6~g NaCl (salt) \par
2.9~g Trisodium citrate dihydrate \par
1.5~g KCl (NoSalt) \par
13.5~g Anhydrous glucose \par
20.5~g Total \par
\end{paracol}
}

\subsection*{Steps}

\paragrcompact{Prepare} Dissolve all ingredients in 1/2~cup hot water. Then add cold water to fill to 1~Liter.

\subsection*{Notes}

\paragrcompact{Substitutions} I purchase \textit{sodium citrate} (for trisodium citrate dihydrate) and \textit{dextrose} instead of glucose.
