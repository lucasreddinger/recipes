Yield: one tray of muffins (12 standard or 6 jumbo).

## Steps

### Preheat
Preheat the oven to 375°F.


### Mix dry ingredients
Combine in a bowl.

- 2 cups flour
- 1 Tbsp baking powder
- 0.5 tsp baking soda
- 0.5 tsp salt

### Cream butter and sugar
In another larger bowl, cream for 3 minutes.

- 1 stick butter
- 2/3 cup sugar

### Add remaining wet ingredients
Add to creamed mixture.

- 2 large eggs
- 1 cup cream
- 1 Tbsp almond extract
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 Tbsp poppy seeds

### Combine
Mix the contents of the wet bowl into the dry bowl until just combined and still lumpy.


### Portion and top
Portion batter into muffin pan, then top muffins with sliced almonds if desired.


### Bake
Bake for 20–22 minutes.
