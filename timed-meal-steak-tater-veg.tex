\vspace{\baselineskip}

crispy potatoes, juicy steak, using only one pan.

start potatoes hot, drop oven for a gentle steak roast, finish with a fast pan sear while asparagus cooks as the steak rests.

\section{0:00 --- preheat + prep}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Preheat oven to \textbf{425\,$^\circ$F}
  \item Wash and dry small potatoes; halve if large
  \item Toss with olive oil, salt, pepper (optional: garlic, rosemary)
  \item Spread in a single layer on a baking sheet
\end{itemize}

\section{0:05 --- roast potatoes}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Roast for \textbf{25--30 min}, flipping halfway
  \item While roasting, season steak with salt and pepper
  \item Trim and prep asparagus
\end{itemize}

\section{0:30 --- remove potatoes, start steak}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Remove potatoes; tent loosely with foil
  \item Lower oven to \textbf{250\,$^\circ$F}
  \item Place steak on a wire rack over a baking sheet
  \item Roast until internal temp reaches:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{115--120\,$^\circ$F} for rare
    \item \textbf{120--125\,$^\circ$F} for medium rare
  \end{itemize}
  \item This typically takes \textbf{20--30 min}
\end{itemize}

\section{0:50 --- heat cast iron}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Heat cast iron pan on high until blazing hot
\end{itemize}

\section{1:00 --- sear steak}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Sear steak for \textbf{45--60 sec} per side
  \item Optional: add butter, smashed garlic, and herbs to baste
  \item Rest steak \textbf{5--10 min}
\end{itemize}

\section{1:00 --- cook asparagus while steak rests}
\vspace{0.2em}
\sfbf{option a: pan-sear (recommended)}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Wipe out cast iron pan
  \item Add olive oil and cook asparagus on medium-high heat
  \item Sauté \textbf{4--6 min}, until just tender
  \item Season with salt, pepper, optional lemon zest or a splash of balsamic
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.2em}
\sfbf{option b: roast}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Toss asparagus with oil, salt, pepper
  \item Roast in hot oven at \textbf{425\,$^\circ$F for 8--10 min}
  \item Do this while steak rests and potatoes reheat
\end{itemize}

\section{1:10 --- plate and serve}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Rewarm potatoes in oven if needed (\textasciitilde5 min)
  \item Slice steak \textbf{against the grain}
  \item Serve steak, potatoes, and asparagus hot
\end{itemize}
