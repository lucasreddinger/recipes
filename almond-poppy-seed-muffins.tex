My attempt at a knock-off of a favorite muffin from a major chain in the U.S. Yield: one tray of muffins (12~standard or 6~jumbo).

\subsection*{Steps}

\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.15}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X l@{}}
\toprule
Preheat the oven to 375°F.
  &
  \mbox{}
\\
\midrule
Combine in a bowl.
  &
  \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}R{2em}@{\hspace{0.3em}}l@{\hspace{0.8em}}l@{}}
  2 & cups & flour \\
  1 & Tbsp & baking powder \\
  1/2 & tsp & baking soda \\
  1/2 & tsp & salt \\
  \end{tabular}
\\
\midrule
In another larger bowl, cream for 3~minutes.
  &
  \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}R{2em}@{\hspace{0.3em}}l@{\hspace{0.8em}}l@{}}
  1 & stick & butter \\
  2/3 & cup & sugar \\
  \end{tabular}
\\
\midrule
Add to creamed mixture.
  &
  \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}R{2em}@{\hspace{0.3em}}l@{\hspace{0.8em}}l@{}}
  2 & \mbox{} & large eggs \\
  1 & cup & cream \\
  1 & Tbsp & almond extract \\
  1 & tsp & vanilla extract \\
  2 & Tbsp & poppy seeds \\
  \end{tabular}
\\
\midrule
Mix the contents of the wet bowl into the dry bowl until just combined and still lumpy.
  &
  \mbox{}
\\
\midrule
Portion batter into muffin pan, then top muffins with sliced almonds if desired.
  &
  \mbox{}
\\
\midrule
Bake for 20--22~minutes.
  &
  \mbox{}
\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\end{center}
