Yield: one tray of muffins (12~standard or 6~jumbo).

\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.15}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X
    R{2em} @{\hspace{0.3em}}
    l @{\hspace{0.8em}}
    l @{}}
\toprule
Preheat the oven to 375°F.
  &  &  &  \\
\midrule
\multirow[t]{4}{\linewidth}{Combine in a bowl.}
  & 2 & cups & flour \\
  & 1 & Tbsp & baking powder \\
  & \nicefrac{1}{2} & tsp & baking soda \\
  & \nicefrac{1}{2} & tsp & salt \\
\midrule
\multirow[t]{2}{\linewidth}{In another larger bowl, cream for 3~minutes.}
  & 1 & stick & butter \\
  & 2/3 & cup & sugar \\
\midrule
\multirow[t]{5}{\linewidth}{Add to creamed mixture.}
  & 2 &  & large eggs \\
  & 1 & cup & cream \\
  & 1 & Tbsp & almond extract \\
  & 1 & tsp & vanilla extract \\
  & 2 & Tbsp & poppy seeds \\
\midrule
Mix the contents of the wet bowl into the dry bowl until just combined and still lumpy.
  &  &  &  \\
\midrule
Portion batter into muffin pan, then top muffins with sliced almonds if desired.
  &  &  &  \\
\midrule
Bake for 20--22~minutes.
  &  &  &  \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\end{center}
