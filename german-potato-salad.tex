\vspace{\baselineskip}

Cold potato salad using mustard and vinegar (no mayonnaise). Potatoes freeze poorly, so this is best kept refrigerated.

\multirow[t]{2}{\linewidth}{When cool enough to handle, peel potatoes. Cut off and discard any brown spots. Chop into 3/4`` to 1'' cubes. Collect potato cubes in a 9x12" glass pan, or a (preferably non-metal) bowl. Pour sauce over still-hot potatoes. As they cool, occasionally mix gently. Once cooled, place in the fridge for at least 12~hours. Remove, drizzle MCT oil over potatoes, and toss. As the oil works into the sauced potatoes, it should become creamy and emulsified. If desired, garnish with minced green onions.}
