Quick spicy cucumber salad. Extra bright and salty for garnishing umami-rich dishes.

\subsection*{Steps}

\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.15}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X l@{}}
\toprule
Thinly slice. Toss with salt and set aside 15~minutes.
  &
  \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}R{2em}@{\hspace{0.3em}}l@{\hspace{0.8em}}l@{}}
  2 & \mbox{} & cucumbers, sliced \\
  1 & t & kosher salt \\
  \end{tabular}
\\
\midrule
Drain well, but do not squeeze. Add remaining ingredients and mix thoroughly. Let sit 5~minutes if desired.
  &
  \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}R{2em}@{\hspace{0.3em}}l@{\hspace{0.8em}}l@{}}
  1 & T & coarse gochugaru \\
  1 & T & chopped scallion \\
  1 & \mbox{} & garlic clove, grated \\
  4 & T & rice vinegar \\
  2 & T & soy sauce \\
  1 & t & sugar \\
  1 & t & toasted sesame seeds \\
  1 & t & toasted sesame oil \\
  \end{tabular}
\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\end{center}
