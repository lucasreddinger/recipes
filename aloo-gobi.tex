Indian potato-cauliflower dish made in an Instant Pot.

\subsection*{Steps}

\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.15}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X l@{}}
\toprule
Saute until cumin seeds brown and mustard seeds pop.
  &
  \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}R{2em}@{\hspace{0.3em}}l@{\hspace{0.8em}}l@{}}
  3 & T & oil \\
  1 & t & cumin seeds \\
  1/2 & t & black mustard seeds \\
  \end{tabular}
\\
\midrule
Add and continue saute for about 6~minutes until onions begin to brown.
  &
  \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}R{2em}@{\hspace{0.3em}}l@{\hspace{0.8em}}l@{}}
  1 & \mbox{} & onion diced \\
  3 & \mbox{} & serrano peppers, minced \\
  \end{tabular}
\\
\midrule
Add and saute for 30~seconds.
  &
  \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}R{2em}@{\hspace{0.3em}}l@{\hspace{0.8em}}l@{}}
  2 & t & minced garlic \\
  2 & t & minced ginger \\
  \end{tabular}
\\
\midrule
Deglaze and mix.
  &
  \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}R{2em}@{\hspace{0.3em}}l@{\hspace{0.8em}}l@{}}
  1/4 & c & water \\
  1 & lb & potatoes, peeled, 1~inch cubes \\
  \end{tabular}
\\
\midrule
Move to Instant Pot (if sauteed elsewhere) and cook at high pressure for 3~minutes then quick-release pressure.
  &
  \mbox{}
\\
\midrule
Add florets. Sprinkle spices thereon. Cook at high pressure for 1~minute then quick-release pressure.
  &
  \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}R{2em}@{\hspace{0.3em}}l@{\hspace{0.8em}}l@{}}
  1 & \mbox{} & cauliflower, cut into florets \\
  1 & t & paprika \\
  1 & t & salt \\
  1 & t & turmeric \\
  1/2 & t & garam masala \\
  1 & t & cayenne \\
  \end{tabular}
\\
\midrule
Remove contents immediately to avoid overcooking! Garnish.
  &
  \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}R{2em}@{\hspace{0.3em}}l@{\hspace{0.8em}}l@{}}
   & \mbox{} & cilantro leaves, chopped \\
  \end{tabular}
\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\end{center}
